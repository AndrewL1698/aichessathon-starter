## Why epoch 36

The run reached epoch 36 at 17:51 on 2026-09-09 and was then found stopped (SIGSTOP, state
`T` in `ps`) with no further progress until 00:10 on 2026-09-10, when it was resumed. With the
upload deadline at 06:00 and a two-hour bench, epoch 36 is exported now; if the resumed run
produces a better epoch in time it will be pushed as a separate file, not over this one.

`--patience 10 --min-delta 1e-5`, 2% split = 5,800,000 rows. 105 to 145 s per epoch awake.
