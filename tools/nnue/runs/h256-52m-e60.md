Best at the last epoch and still falling at about −1.4e-5 per epoch. Full curve in
`h256-52m-e60-history.json`.

**Run-to-run noise:** this run's epoch 30 reads 0.014808 where the 30-epoch run, same seed and
data, read 0.014886. MPS is not bit-deterministic, so differences under about 1e-4 between runs
are noise. The 60-epoch gain (0.014886 to 0.014602) is about three times that.

Sanity table after epoch 60 (White to move):

```
start +29cp  |  +1 pawn (white) +83cp  |  +1 rook (white) +435cp  |  mate in 1 (white) +2005cp
```

The mate-in-1 number is the ±2000 label clip; the net has learned that a queen next to a bare
king at the edge is at the clip, which is correct for a static score in that position.

## Export

7,374; 272,955 bytes. The headroom keeps shrinking with training as layer-1 weights grow.

