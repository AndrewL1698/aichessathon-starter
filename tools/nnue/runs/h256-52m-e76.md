## Why the exported epoch is 76, not 119

The layer-1 weights keep growing with training, and the int16 accumulator bound at qa=256
(32 largest |weights| per neuron plus the bias) crosses 32767 after epoch 78. Per the
engine session's constraint the export does not drop to qa=128 (the int-versus-float error
is already near the 25 cp gate at 256 and halving the scale doubles it), so the file is the
**best-val epoch among those that fit at qa=256: epoch 76**, val 0.014528, bound 32029.
Epoch 78 is the last that fits. Epoch 119 would need qa=196.

Epoch 76's 0.014528 is 7e-5 below the [e60](h256-52m-e60.md) file, inside the ~1e-4
run-to-run noise on MPS, so treat the two as equivalent until the bench separates them.
